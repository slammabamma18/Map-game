Little Worlds

A world in your hands. Paint land and sea, plant forests, and drop humans, elves, orcs and dwarves. They will hunt, cut wood, build houses one at a time, found villages, crown kings and go to war, all on their own.

Then do what gods do: lightning, fire, meteors, plague, blessing, rain. Tap any creature, village or kingdom to see who they are.
